Help & Scheduling Logic

Plan bounded work, record actual activity, and review assessment evidence separately

Activity Scope and Overrides

Standard scope has 11 revision activities for Both and 9 for Prelims Only or Mains Only, before overrides. Compact scope filters the activity list. Subject defaults and explicit topic stages can change the resolved list; explicit stage selections take precedence over the preset. Review the topic's plan rather than assuming a fixed count. Subject profiles describe practice outputs; they do not automatically switch exam modes. Pipeline completion means the selected activities were marked done, not mastery or exam readiness.

Bounded Sources and Syllabus Mapping

In Strategy & Evidence, choose a subject profile and bounded foundation, spine, or depth resources. Record chapters or scope, and use depth reading for a named gap or question. Verify your syllabus entries against the current official syllabus before marking the list verified, then map them to topics. Coverage counts mapped topics with learning completed; it does not verify factual accuracy, exhaustive coverage, or retention. No PDFs are imported automatically, and source facts are not validated by the tracker.

Actual Logs, Not Elapsed Time

Log study hours actually spent and mark activities only when completed. Elapsed calendar days are not evidence of study progress. Learning-hour estimates and pipeline dates are planning aids, separate from assessment results. Completing a revision or logging hours does not by itself establish recall, accuracy, or writing quality.

Shared Capacity and Scheduling Warnings

Existing work and future projections use shared daily capacity for learning, revisions, and cumulative sessions. Activity durations, revision intervals, and study budgets affect the plan. The scheduler searches a finite horizon; impossible durations, capacity conflicts, or unmet constraints can leave work Not scheduled. Review warnings and adjust the plan instead of treating missing dates as a promise. Unallocated work is not an automatic rollover or a reserved slot tomorrow.

Assessment Evidence and Training Goals

Record MCQ attempts, writing, delayed recall, and subject-skill checks separately from activity completion. Externally evaluated writing is distinct from self-assessed or unreviewed work. Diagnostic or repeated MCQ sets are diagnostic-only; sample size, timing, delayed recall, and evidence age affect the displayed status. Previously on-target evidence can become stale. Subject-skill checks are records, not automatic readiness ratings. Training goals are configurable personal practice targets, not official cutoffs or guarantees of exam performance.

Error Repairs

Tagged assessment errors create repair items for follow-up. Record the correction and retest outcome when resolving an item. A repair due date is a reminder, not automatically reserved study time; fit the work into your available budget. Repair records do not provide fully adaptive rescheduling of the study plan.

Current Affairs Links Before Starting

Use a topic's CA Links action to attach and review relevant current-affairs sources even before starting learning. Check source dates and factual claims yourself. Links are references, not an extra fixed revision stage or proof of readiness.
